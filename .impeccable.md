## Design Context

### Users
Adults completing a private health and lifestyle screening, often on a phone and without clinical training. Their job is to understand each question, answer confidently, pause and resume safely, and leave with a clear, non-diagnostic summary and practical next steps.

### Brand Personality
Calm, friendly, and trustworthy. The experience should reduce anxiety and feel human without becoming childish. Miko is a warm guide inspired by the companionship of Duolingo-style characters: present throughout the journey, visibly animated, and always available for plain-language help.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined, minimal light interface with warm paper neutrals, deep health-green typography, restrained coral and amber accents, generous breathing room, and soft rounded controls. The character provides the memorable visual signature. Avoid dense dashboards, excessive cards, clinical sterility, neon gradients, and decorative visual noise.

### Design Principles
1. Keep one obvious action and one clear idea in focus at a time.
2. Keep Miko visible and helpful throughout onboarding, questions, and results without covering essential controls.
3. Make health logic, privacy boundaries, validation, and safety language unambiguous.
4. Use calm motion only to explain state or bring Miko to life; respect reduced-motion settings.
5. Design mobile-first with accessible contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus, and resilient multilingual layouts.
