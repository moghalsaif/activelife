    Clear by design

    How the assessment logic works

    01

    Each answer earns 3, 2, or 1. The most health-supportive answer receives 3, an uncertain or middle answer 2, and a risk answer 1.

    02

    Most areas use a weighted average. A perfect area is “Very satisfactory”; 66% or higher is “Satisfactory”; below that is “Needs attention.”

    03

    Safety-critical areas are stricter. Cardiac, exercise risk, severe stress symptoms/crisis, dependency, cancer, HIV, and safety can be flagged by one risk answer.

    04

    The overall result has a safety gate. A score cannot be called satisfactory when cardiac, cancer, HIV, or safety contains a flagged risk answer.

    Clinical safeguard

    The document also includes an older heart-attack percentage formula. This prototype does not present that as a modern medical prediction; doing so would require clinical validation and current medical oversight.
